Soul Links

Soul Linked Items are special magical items that are bound to an
adventurer early on in their career. These items grow in power with the
adventurer and are highly unique, usually acting as manifestations of an
adventurer’s personality. If this item is lost, the adventurer must
perform an intensive ritual or go on a grand journey to recover it or
manifest a new one, the details of such are determined by the manner in
which it was lost and the nature of the adventurer who wielded it. When
making your Soul Link, pick a magic item type, this can be an Arcane
Focus, Armor, Shield, Tool, Wondrous Item, or Weapon. Each magic item
type has unique features that can be chosen as the item grows in power.

When you gain a Soul Link, choose a level 1 Manifestation. As you gain
levels, so will your Soul Link. At levels 4, 8, 12, 16, and 19, your
item will manifest newer and more powerful abilities. When you reach a
level where you gain a feature, you can choose any feature that has a
level requirement less than or equal to your level. Choose any
Manifestation from either the general list (which applies to any type of
magical item) or one from the specific list associated with your item
type. Also feel free to get creative and suggest a manifestation
specific to your item. Any suggestions are subject to DM approval and
should generally line up with other manifestation options.

Flavoring your Soul Link

When considering what kind of Soul Link your character has, carefully
consider how this item might look, any quirks or oddities that make it
unique, how your character acquired such an item, if the item has a
history with any previous owners, what it might look or sound like when
using its manifestations, what personality traits it has if it is
sentient, and so much more. Try to integrate your Soul Link with your
character's personality, backstory, and fighting style. Try to go beyond
just “I found this sword one day and it has cool powers I guess”. Really
dive into features and descriptions that enhance the roleplaying
elements of your character. Suggest different abilities that might be
unique to your item, think of a colorful description, make up a cool
name, or even add curses and disadvantages your character might have to
overcome. Get creative and make a magic item that really tells a story.

### General

Manifestations in this list are fairly common and not tied to any
specific item type. They can still add flavor to your character, but are
just as at home on a suit of armor, as they  are on a dagger, lute, or
magic wand.

#### Manifestations

##### Level 1:

- Glow

<!-- -->

- The item pulses with your heartbeat, casting warm light or a cold
  glow. The item emits Bright Light in a 20-foot radius and Dim Light
  for an additional 20 feet. You can beckon or snuff out this light as a
  Bonus Action.

<!-- -->

- Pocket Space

<!-- -->

- While holding the item, you can send it to an extradimensional space
  as a Bonus Action. You can retrieve the item from the extradimensional
  space when you roll Initiative or as a Bonus Action. You cannot
  retrieve the item if you don’t have a free hand to hold it.

<!-- -->

- \*Variation for Armor: Retractable

<!-- -->

- You can don and doff this armor as a Bonus Action

<!-- -->

- Disguise

<!-- -->

- As a Bonus Action, you can disguise your item into a similarly sized
  mundane item. Choose the mundane appearance when you select this
  feature. While in mundane form, the item doesn't appear magical and
  only a spell of 2nd level or higher reveals otherwise.

<!-- -->

- Sentience

<!-- -->

- Not all Soul Links are sentient, but it is far from uncommon. A
  sentient soul link has its own personality that compliments the
  personality of its wielder, either by encouraging them to stay on the
  true and right path, or tempting them to wander on the darker roads.
  Either way, having a sentient Soul Link provides the following
  benefit:

<!-- -->

- Your Soul Link gains an Intelligence, Wisdom, and Charisma score.
  Choose one to start at 8, another to start at 10, and the final to
  start at 12. Whenever you gain a new manifestation, you can choose to
  forgo gaining a manifestation to increase one of these scores by 2.
  Whenever you make a skill check or saving throw using any of these
  ability scores, you can instead choose to use your soul link’s ability
  score. Apply any of your proficiencies or class features after
  changing which ability score you are using.

##### Level 4:

- Blinding Flash (Requires Glow)

<!-- -->

- You cause a bright flash of light in a 20 foot Emanation around you.
  Each creature of your choice in the area must make a DC 14
  Constitution saving throw or take 2d8 Radiant damage and have the
  Blinded condition until the end of its next turn. You can use this
  feature 2 times and regain all expended uses when you finish a Long
  Rest.

<!-- -->

- Spellwrought I

<!-- -->

- Choose 1 Cantrip from the Cleric, Druid, or Wizard spell list. While
  holding this item, you can cast that cantrip.
- Choose 2 first level spells or 1 second level spell from the same
  spell list that you chose the cantrip from. You can cast each spell
  once while holding this item and regain the ability to do so when you
  finish a Long Rest.

<!-- -->

- The spellcasting ability for these spells depends on the item type you
  chose

|                    |                                                        |
|--------------------|--------------------------------------------------------|
| Item Type          | Spellcasting ability                                   |
| Arcane Focus       | Same as your spellcasting class                        |
| Armor/Shield       | Constitution                                           |
| Tool/Wondrous item | Intelligence/Wisdom/Charisma                           |
| Weapon             | Same as used for the attack rolls made with the weapon |

##### Level 12:

- Spellwrought II (Requires Spellwrought I)

<!-- -->

- Choose 1 third level spell or 1 fourth level spell from the same spell
  list that you chose the original spells from. You can cast either
  spell once while holding this item and regain the ability to do so
  when you finish a Long Rest. Use the same spellcasting ability that
  you use for Spellwrought I.

##### Level 16:

- Spellwrought III (Requires Spellwrought II)

<!-- -->

- Choose 1 fifth level spell from the same spell list that you chose the
  original spells from. You can cast the spell once while holding this
  item and regain the ability to do so when you finish a Long Rest. Use
  the same spellcasting ability that you use for Spellwrought I.

### Arcane Focus

Arcane Focus items represent any item that a spellcaster might use to
channel their magical power. This can be a wand, staff, orb, holy
symbol, druidic focus, or another item suitable for the type of magic
that spellcaster is using.

#### Manifestations

##### Level 1:

- Cantrip Loan

<!-- -->

- You can try to cast a cantrip that you don't know. The cantrip must be
  on your spell list, and you must make a DC 10 Intelligence (Arcana)
  check. If the check succeeds, you cast the spell. If the check fails,
  so does the spell, and the action used to cast the spell is wasted.

<!-- -->

- Adaptable

<!-- -->

- While holding this item, you can change one or your prepared spells
  for another one on your spell list when you finish a Short or Long
  Rest.

<!-- -->

- Battle Focus

<!-- -->

- You can attach the focus to a weapon or shield by pressing the focus
  against it for at least 10 minutes.Thereafter, the focus cannot be
  removed unless you detach it as an action or the weapon is destroyed.
  Not even an Antimagic Field causes it to fall off. While attached in
  this way, you can use the weapon or shield as an extension of the
  focus, allowing you to use any property of the item while holding the
  attached weapon or shield.

<!-- -->

- Mystic Lens

<!-- -->

- As a Bonus action, you can open your mind to the weave, granting
  yourself Advantage on Arcana checks for 10 minutes. Once you use this
  feature, you must finish a Long Rest before doing so again.

<!-- -->

- Arcane Resonance

<!-- -->

- When you cast a spell using your highest available spell slot, you can
  grant a creature within 30 feet of you Temporary hit points equal to
  twice the spells level.

##### Level 4:

- Alter Spells

<!-- -->

- You learn two Metamagic options of your choice from the sorcerer
  class. You can use only one Metamagic option on a spell when you cast
  it, unless the option says otherwise. Whenever you reach a level that
  grants the Ability Score Improvement feature, you can replace one of
  these Metamagic options with another one from the sorcerer class.
- You gain 2 sorcery points to spend on Metamagic (these points are
  added to any sorcery points you have from another source but can be
  used only on Metamagic). You regain all spent sorcery points when you
  finish a long rest.

<!-- -->

- Spell Storing

<!-- -->

- This item stores spells cast into it, holding them until the wielder
  uses them. The item can store up to 3 levels worth of spells at a
  time. Any creature can cast a spell of level 1 through 3 into the item
  by touching the item as the spell is cast. The spell has no effect
  other than to be stored in the item. If the item can’t hold the spell,
  the spell is expended without effect. The level of the slot used to
  cast the spell determines how much space it uses. While holding this
  item, you can cast any spell stored in it. The spell uses the slot
  level, spell save DC, spell attack bonus, and spellcasting ability of
  the original caster but is otherwise treated as if you cast the spell.
  The spell cast from the item is no longer stored in it, freeing up
  space.

<!-- -->

- Enhanced I

<!-- -->

- This focus grants a +1 bonus to your spell save DC and spell attack
  rolls.

<!-- -->

- Empowered I

<!-- -->

- When you cast a spell that deals damage or restores hitpoints, you can
  add 1d4 to one damage or healing roll against one target of that
  spell.

<!-- -->

- Spell Redirect

<!-- -->

- When you are targeted by a spell that only targets you, you can use
  your Reaction to redirect the spell to a different creature within the
  spell’s range. When you do so, the new target makes a Dexterity Saving
  throw against your spell save DC. On a failed save the spell is
  redirected to them. On a successful save, you remain as the target of
  the spell. Once you use this feature, you must finish a Long Rest
  before doing so again.

##### Level 8:

- Arcane Surge (requires Alter Spells or at least 2 levels in Sorcerer)

<!-- -->

- When you take a Short Rest, you can regain up to 2 of your expended
  sorcery points.

##### Level 12:

- Enhanced II (Requires Enhanced I)

<!-- -->

- This focus grants a +2 bonus to your spell save DC and spell attack
  rolls. (instead of +1)

<!-- -->

- Empowered II (Required Empowered I)

<!-- -->

- When you cast a spell that deals damage or restores hitpoints, you can
  add 2d4 to one damage or healing roll against one target of that
  spell. (instead of 1d4)

##### Level 16:

- Enhanced III (Requires Enhanced II)

<!-- -->

- This focus grants a +3 bonus to your spell save DC and spell attack
  rolls. (instead of +2)

<!-- -->

- Empowered III (Required Empowered II)

<!-- -->

- When you cast a spell that deals damage or restores hitpoints, you can
  add 3d4 to one damage or healing roll against one target of that
  spell. (instead of 2d4)

### Armor

Armors are a protective layer that many adventurers use to shield
themselves from harm. Refer to the equipment section of the PHB to see
all of the armor options.

#### Manifestations

##### Level 1:

- Lightweight

<!-- -->

- You ignore the Strength requirement for this armor.

<!-- -->

- Quiet

<!-- -->

- You ignore the Disadvantage on Stealth checks imposed by this armor.

<!-- -->

- Swift

<!-- -->

- While wearing this armor, your Speed increases by 10 feet.

<!-- -->

- Climbing

<!-- -->

- While wearing this armor, you have a climbing speed equal to your
  Speed.

<!-- -->

- Swimming

<!-- -->

- While wearing this armor, you have a swimming speed equal to your
  Speed.

<!-- -->

- Dig In

<!-- -->

- Whenever you are moved against your will, you can use your Reaction to
  reduce the distance moved by 10 feet.

##### Level 4:

- Aquatic (Requires Swimming)

<!-- -->

- While wearing this armor, you can breathe normally underwater and can
  cast Shape Water without requiring verbal or somatic components.
  Additionally you can take the Dash action as a Bonus action while
  Swimming.

<!-- -->

- Verticality  (Requires Climbing)

<!-- -->

- While wearing this armor, you can cast Spider Climb on yourself
  without requiring a spell slot, concentration, material, verbal, or
  somatic components. Additionally, whenever you take damage from
  falling, you instead take half damage.

<!-- -->

- Sturdy

<!-- -->

- Whenever you roll Initiative or when you finish a Short or Long Rest,
  you gain a number of Temporary Hitpoints equal to your Level.

##### Level 8:

- Enhanced I

<!-- -->

- This armor provides a +1 bonus to AC.

<!-- -->

- Resistance

<!-- -->

- You gain Resistance to one damage type other than Force.

<!-- -->

- Reeling

<!-- -->

- When a creature hits you with an attack roll, you can spend your
  reaction to force that creature to subtract 1 from all its attack
  rolls before the start or the next turn.

##### Level 12:

- Last Stand

<!-- -->

- If you drop to 0 Hit Points and don’t die outright, you can make a DC
  10 Constitution saving throw. If you succeed, your Hit Points instead
  change to a number equal to your Armor Class plus your Level. Every
  time you use this ability your Armor Class decreases by 1 and the DC
  for the Constitution check used in this ability increases by 5.

##### Level 16:

- Enhanced II (Requires Enhanced I)

<!-- -->

- This armor provides a +2 bonus to AC. (instead of +1)

<!-- -->

- Immunity (Requires Resistance)

<!-- -->

- You gain Immunity to the damage type you chose to gain resistance to,
  using the resistance feature.

##### Level 19:

- Enhanced III (Requires Enhanced II)

<!-- -->

- This armor provides a +3 bonus to AC. (instead of +2)

### Shield

Shields are an item that some adventurers carry to give them an extra
boost of protection, at the cost of a free hand.

#### Manifestations

##### Level 1:

- Weaponized

<!-- -->

- This shield has a built in weapon. Choose either Melee or Ranged. To
  use the associated mastery property, you must choose this built in
  weapon as one of your weapon masteries, if you have the weapon mastery
  feature.

<!-- -->

- Melee: STR Attack, 1d6 Bludgeoning/Piercing/Slashing (choose 1 when
  you select this feature), Mastery: Push
- Ranged: DEX (20/60) Thrown Attack, 1d4 Bludgeoning/Piercing/Slashing
  (choose 1 when you select this feature), Mastery: Slow

<!-- -->

- Reflective

<!-- -->

- You have Advantage on saving throws against being Blinded.

<!-- -->

- Sigil Shield

<!-- -->

- You can magically inscribe a symbol on the shield that glows when a
  type of creature is within 60 feet (undead, fiends, etc.—choose one).
  When you finish a Long Rest, you can change the creature type.

##### Level 4:

- Shared Protection

<!-- -->

- When a creature within 30 feet of you other than you is hit by an
  attack roll, you can take a Reaction to grant that target a bonus
  their AC equal to this shield’s AC bonus. This bonus lasts until the
  start of your next turn.

<!-- -->

- Enhanced I

<!-- -->

- This shield grants a +1 bonus to your AC while wielding it.

<!-- -->

- Defensive Pulse

<!-- -->

- When reduced to half of your maximum hitpoints or less, your shield
  emits a burst of force. Creatures of your choice within 10 feet must
  succeed a Dexterity saving throw or be pushed back 10 feet. Once you
  use the feature, you must regain all your hitpoints before you can use
  it again.

<!-- -->

- Greater Weapon (Requires Weaponized)

<!-- -->

- Your Shield’s built in weapon grows in power.

<!-- -->

- Melee: The damage of this weapon increases to 1d10 and it gains the
  Topple Mastery Property.
- Ranged: The damage of this weapon increases to 1d8 and it gains the
  Return property.

##### Level 8:

- Counter (Requires Weaponized)

<!-- -->

- When a creature within your Weaponized Shield’s range misses you with
  an attack, you can take a Reaction to make an attack against that
  creature using your shield’s built in weapon.

<!-- -->

- Defensive Aegis

<!-- -->

- When you are hit by an attack, you can interpose your shield, reducing
  the damage by 3d10. You can use this feature twice and regain the
  ability to do so when you finish a Long Rest.

##### Level 12:

- Shield Dome

<!-- -->

- While holding your shield you can cast Wall of Force without requiring
  a spell slot, verbal, somatic, or material components. When you cast
  it in this way, the spell forms into a hemispherical dome or globe
  that has a radius of 10 feet and is centered on you. The Wall of Force
  does not move with you when you cast it in this way. Once you
  cast Wall of Force in this way, you must finish a Long Rest before
  doing so again.

<!-- -->

- Enhanced II (Requires Enhanced I)

<!-- -->

- This shield grants a +2 bonus to your AC while wielding it. (instead
  of +1)

##### Level 16:

- Enhanced III (Requires Enhanced II)

<!-- -->

- This shield grants a +3 bonus to your AC while wielding it. (instead
  of +2)

### Tool

Most tools are sets of items used for creation of items, music, or (in
the case of thieves tools) “clever solutions”.

#### Manifestations

##### Level 1:

- Handy

<!-- -->

- You gain a +1 to ability checks that use this tool.

<!-- -->

- Amorphous

<!-- -->

- As an action, you can change this tool into any other tool that you
  are proficient with.

<!-- -->

- Repair (Artisan’s toll only)

<!-- -->

- You can cast Mending while holding this tool.

<!-- -->

- Spectacle (Instrument only)

<!-- -->

- You can cast Prestidigiation while playing this instrument.

<!-- -->

- Diversion (Thieve’s tools only)

<!-- -->

- You can cast Minor Illusion while holding this tool.

<!-- -->

- Sample (Poisoner’s Kit only)

<!-- -->

- You can cast Poison Spray while holding this tool.

##### Level 4:

- Watchful

<!-- -->

- You can cast Find Traps without requiring a spell slot while holding
  this tool. Use the ability associated with your tool as the
  spellcasting ability for this spell.

<!-- -->

- Quick Craft

<!-- -->

- Whenever you use this tool to create a non-magical object, you can
  spend half of the time that it would normally require.

<!-- -->

- Frugal Craft

<!-- -->

- Whenever you use this tool to create a non-magical object, you can
  spend half of the gold or materials that it would normally require.

##### Level 8:

- Quick Enchant (requires Quick Craft)

<!-- -->

- Whenever you use this tool to create a magical object, you can spend
  half of the time that it would normally require.

<!-- -->

- Frugal Enchant (requires Frugal Craft)

<!-- -->

- Whenever you use this tool to create a magical object, you can spend
  half of the gold or materials that it would normally require.

<!-- -->

- Artiface

<!-- -->

- Choose one Artificer Infusion. You can use this infusion using the
  same rules described in the Artificer class. You may only choose
  infusions if they do not have a level requirement.

##### Level 12:

- Fabrication

<!-- -->

- You can cast Fabricate using this tool. Use the ability associated
  with your tool as the spellcasting ability for this spell. Once you
  use this feature, you must finish a Long Rest before you can do so
  again.

##### Level 16:

- Animation

<!-- -->

- You can cast Animate Objects using this tool. Use the ability
  associated with your tool as the spellcasting ability for this spell.
  Once you use this feature, you must finish a Long Rest before you can
  do so again.

### Weapon

#### Manifestations

##### Level 1:

- Masterful

<!-- -->

- This weapon gains one more Mastery property of your choice. You can
  only use one mastery property at a time. You only need to have this
  weapon on your list of weapon masteries once to be able to use both
  properties.

<!-- -->

- Loyalty (Thrown weapons only)

<!-- -->

- This weapon returns to your hand immediately after it hits or misses
  its target

<!-- -->

- Bottomless Quiver (Ammunition weapons only)

<!-- -->

- This weapon does not need to be loaded with ammunition, creating its
  own instead when fired.

<!-- -->

- Power Strike

<!-- -->

- When you hit an attack with this weapon, you can choose to deal an
  extra 1d8 damage of the weapons damage type. You can use this feature
  twice and regain the ability to do so when you finish a Short or Long
  rest.

<!-- -->

- Affinity

<!-- -->

- This weapon deals one of the following damage types instead of its
  normal damage type: Acid, Cold, Fire, Lightning, Necrotic, Poison,
  Psychic, Radiant, Thunder

##### Level 4:

<!-- -->

##### Level 12:

- Empowered II (Requires Empowered I)

- Reeling

##### Level 16:

  type is the same as the weapon normally deals. (instead of 2d4)

### Wondrous Item

Wondrous Items are the most varied category of magic items. They are
usually mundane objects whose capabilities have been magically enhanced.

#### Manifestations

##### Level 1:

- Choose the ability of one common magic item (must be listed as a
  wondrous item)

##### Level 4:

- Choose the ability of one uncommon magic item (must be listed as a
  wondrous item)

##### Level 8:

- Choose the ability of one uncommon magic item (must be listed as a
  wondrous item)

##### Level 12:

- Choose the ability of one rare magic item (must be listed as a
  wondrous item)

##### Level 16:

- Choose the ability of one very rare magic item (must be listed as a
  wondrous item)

##### Level 19:

- Choose the ability of one legendary magic item (must be listed as a
  wondrous item)
