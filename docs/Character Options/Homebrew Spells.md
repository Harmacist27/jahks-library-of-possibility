#### Phantom Blade

##### Illusion Cantrip (Bard, Sorcerer, Warlock, Wizard)

Casting Time: Action

Range: 60 feet

Component: S, M (a melee weapon with which you have proficiency that is
worth 1+ CP)

Duration: Instantaneous

As you strike with your weapon, you weave a phantom image around it,
extending the weapon’s reach. Make one attack with the weapon, when you
do so, your reach is increased by 5 feet for this attack.

Cantrip Upgrade. When you hit the attack made with this spell, the
attack deals extra psychic damage when you reach levels 5 (1d8), 11
(2d8), 17 (3d8).

#### Eddie’s Iron Maiden

##### 4th-level Conjuration Spell (Bard, Sorcerer, Warlock, Wizard)

Casting Time: Action

Range: 60 feet

Component: V

Duration: Concentration, up to 1 minute

You conjure an Iron Maiden around a creature you can see within range.
The target must make a Strength saving throw or take 4d10 piercing
damage and be trapped in the Iron Maiden. While trapped, the target has
the Blinded and Restrained conditions. While trapped the creature has
total cover from every direction and takes 2d10 piercing damage at the
start of each of its turns. At the end of each of its turns, the
affected target repeats the save, ending the spell on itself on a
success.

Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot. When you cast this spell with a spell
slot of 5th level or higher, the initial damage of the spell increases
by 1d10 for each spell level above 4.

#### Jahk’s First Lesson

##### 1st-level Evocation Spell (Wizard)

Casting Time: Bonus Action, which you take immediately after hitting a
target with a melee weapon or an Unarmed Strike

Range: Self

Component: V

Duration: 1 minute

As you hit the target, you teach an ally a short fact or proverb. The
target takes add extra 1d8 radiant damage. Until the spell ends, the
ally you taught can add 1d8 to the damage they deal with an attack or
spell. The damage type is the same as the attack or spell that it is
added to. Once the d8 is used, the spell ends.

Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot. When you cast this spell with a spell
slot of 2nd level or higher, the number of allies you can teach
increases by 1 for each slot level above 1st.

#### Corpse Explosion

##### 3rd-level Necromancy Spell (Necromancer, Wizard)

Casting Time: Action

Range: 150 feet

Component: V, S, M (a small vial of putrid methane gas or a piece of
rotting flesh)

Duration: Instantaneous

You send a concentrated burst of necrotic energy toward a corpse or
undead creature you can see within range, causing it to unleash a
devastating explosion.

All creatures within a 10 foot Emmination of the target (including the
target) must succeed on a Dexterity saving throw as the target explodes,
taking 6d8 Force damage on a failed save or half as much damage on a
successful one. If the original target was a corpse or if it is reduced
to 0 hit points by this damage, it and everything non magical it is
wearing and carrying are disintegrated into gray dust.

Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot. For each spell slot level above 3, the
Force damage increases by 1d8 and the radius of the Emmination increases
by 5 feet.
