<span >Casting
Time:</span><span > Action</span>

<span >Range:</span><span > 60
feet</span>

<span >Component:</span><span > V</span>

<span >Duration:</span><span > Concentration,
up to 1 minute</span>

<span >You
conjure an Iron Maiden around a creature you can see within range. The
target must make a Strength saving throw or take 4d10 piercing damage
