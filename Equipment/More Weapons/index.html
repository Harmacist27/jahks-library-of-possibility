    Weapon Properties:¶

    Return: A weapon that has the Return property returns to the wielder’s hands after hits or misses a target that it was thrown at.

    Ultra Heavy: You have Disadvantage on attack rolls with an Ultra Heavy weapon if it’s a Melee weapon and your Strength score isn’t at least 15 or if it’s a Ranged weapon and your Dexterity score isn’t at least 15.
