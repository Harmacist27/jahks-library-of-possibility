    • Boots of Bounding
    • These boots allow you to jump up to 100 feet in any direction.
    • Cape of Gliding
    • This cape allows you to descend 5 feet horizontally for each food you fall vertically. You cannot fall faster than 10 feet in a round while using this feature.  
    • Cobra’s Fangs
    • Set of two daggers that deal an additional 1d6 poison damage. They return to your hands after throwing them. Any target hit by one of the daggers must succeed on a DC 12 Constitution saving throw or be paralyzed for 1 minute. The target can repeat this saving throw at the end of their next turn.
    • The Arbalest of Storm
    • Ranged Weapon, 5 charges, 3 for lightning arrow, 1 for hail of thorns, no ammo required, thunder damage instead of piercing damage.
    • Exploding Sword
    • Can cast booming blade
    • 5 charges, refresh at dawn daily. After landing a successful attack, you can cause the sword to evoke a violent explosion. Any creature within 10 feet of the target (including you) must make a DC 15 Dexterity Saving Throw or take 4d6 thunder damage. On a successful save, creatures take half damage.
    • Rust Monster Armor
    • Any medium or heavy armor
    • Rust Monster rust metal ability
    • +1 AC
    • Spell-Might Gloves
    • While equipped, any time you cast a spell that requires a spell attack, you can take a -5 penalty to the attack roll, but if the attack hits, it deals an extra 1d8 damage.
    • Vinewood Sword
    • This is a +1 wooden longsword. From this sword, you can cast Ensnaring Strike without using a spell slot. The spell save DC for this is 13 or your spell save DC, whichever is higher. You can cast this spell in this way 3 times per daily dawn.
    • Hardwood Shield
    • This is a +1 wooden shield. As a reaction when you take damage, you can entomb yourself in bark. You gain 50 temporary hit points, which take as much of the triggering damage as possible. Immediately after you take the damage, you gain vulnerability to fire damage, your speed is reduced to 0, and you are incapacitated. These effects, including any remaining temporary hit points, all end at the end of your next turn or when the temporary hit points are depleted.
    • Staff of Arboreal Power
    • Once per daily dawn, you can cause a tree to sprout from the ground at a point on the ground within 60 feet of you. The tree sprouts quickly, occupying a 5 foot radius cylinder that is 20 feet high. Any creature in the area at the time the tree sprouts must make a DC 13 Dexterity saving throw or take 3d8 bludgeoning damage and be thrust 25 feet into the air. If a creature succeeds on the save, they move to the closest square outside of the space that the tree occupies. The tree disappears after 8 hours or immediately after it takes any damage.
    • Gear Axe
    • The gear axe is a greataxe with 2 large, sharp toothed gears instead of blades. You can use a bonus action to cause the gears to rapidly spin for one minute, dealing an 3d6 slashing damage to any target. While the blades are not spinning, the damage of the axe is only 1d8 bludgeoning. Once this feature has been used, the axe must be repaired by spending 10 minutes using tinkers tools or smith’s tools to fix it. Proficiency in either tool reduces this time to 1 minute.
    • Damage
    • While spinning: 3d6
    • While off: 1d8
    • Power Flail
    • This flail has magical enhancements that allow it to propel its head forward. When you take the attack action on your turn, you can replace one of your attacks with a magically propelled strike. The flail’s head launches in a straight line from you up to 30 feet in a direction you choose before falling to the ground, stopping early if it impacts against a solid surface. If the object would strike a creature, that creature must make a DC 14 Dexterity saving throw. On a failed save, the head strikes the target and stops moving. Whatever the head strikes takes 2d10 bludgeoning damage. When you use this ability, roll a d20, if the number is lower than the damage rolled, this feature can’t be used until the weapon is repaired. The flail must be repaired by spending 10 minutes using tinkers tools or smith’s tools to fix it. Proficiency in either tool reduces this time to 1 minute.
    • Trifold Rapier
    • This rapier’s blade is made of intricate parts that allow it to telescopically fold into and out of the handle. Extending or retracting the blade can be done as an action, or as part of an attack you make with this weapon. Additionally, the blade is made of three sections that can spread apart to cause severe damage while inside an enemy. After hitting an enemy with an attack using this weapon, you can cause the blade to open up, increasing the size of the puncture wound. When you do so, the target must make a DC 14 Constitution saving throw or take an additional 1d8 slashing damage and have their movement speed slowed by 15ft until the end of their next turn. On a successful save, the target takes half the damage and isn’t slowed. Once this feature has been used 3 times, it can’t be used until the weapon is repaired. The rapier must be repaired by spending 10 minutes using tinkers tools or smith’s tools to fix it. Proficiency in either tool reduces this time to 1 minute.
    • BFC (Big Fucking Crossbow)
    • This crossbow requires a bonus action to reload. When you fire it, choose any number of creatures that you can see in a 100 ft line. Make an attack roll against the first creature in the line. If the attack lands, it takes 3d8 piercing damage. For each subsequent creature in the line, use the same roll to attack for each target. If the attack misses a given target’s AC, the target takes no damage but the bolt continues on its path. When you fire the crossbow, roll a d6. On a roll of 1, the BFC cannot be used until it is repaired. The BFC must be repaired by spending 10 minutes using tinkers tools or smith’s tools to fix it. Proficiency in either tool reduces this time to 1 minute.
    • Alchemist's Blowgun
    • This is a custom blowgun designed to work with potions. The blowgun uses special ammunition that have built in glass vials to store potions. The blowgun magically summons its ammunition into a special compartment that is attached to the side, so no ammo needs to be purchased for this weapon. This counts as a rare magic weapon. This weapon has a +2 to attack rolls made with it. When a potion is applied to this weapon’s ammunition, the save DC for any effect caused by the potion used is increased by 1. 3 times per daily dawn, you can forgo any attack roll and instead choose to automatically hit one target. This effect is blocked by the shield spell.
    • Cube of Chaos
    • This tiny cube has rotating sides that change the orientation of colored squares on the face of the cube. Throwing the cube creates a random magical effect. The cube can be thrown up to 60ft to a point you can see within range. The cube can be used once per daily dawn. Putting all the same colored squares on the same face of the cube requires a DC 18 intelligence check or 2 hours of experimentation and pondering. Once the cube has been solved, the user can choose the effect instead of rolling on the table. If the cube was solved and then thrown, its faces shuffle and it needs to be solved again before it can be used in this way. If a creature solves the cube 5 times, they no longer need to make a skill check to solve it, and it only takes 1 minute.
    d6 Effect
    1 (White) The ground within a 20ft cube centered on the cube becomes frozen. Any creature that moves onto or across the ice must make a DC 16 Dexterity saving throw or fall prone.
    2 (Green) Vines, shrubs and other plants grow in a 50 ft cube centered at the cube, causing the ground in that area to become difficult terrain.
    3 (Blue) All creatures within a 15 ft cube centered on the cube that have less than 30 hit points fall asleep for 1 minute, until they are woken up or take damage.
    4 (Red) All creatures of your choice within a 30 ft cube centered on the cube regain (1d4)3 hit points.
    5 (Orange) Fireball is cast, entered on the cube, but instead of a sphere, it is a cube with sides of length 40 ft, and centered on the cube. The save DC for this fireball is 16
    6 (Yellow) Sun beam is cast in a 60ft line. The direction of the beam is random. Roll a d4, on a 1, the beam points north, 2 for east, 3 for south, and 4 for west. If the cube is solved, the direction is not random

    • Coin of Luck
    • Flip the coin and target one individual. When you do so, call heads or tails. If the coin lands in your favor, a Wild Magic Surge is triggered on the target. If the coin lands in their favor, a Wild Magic Surge is triggered on you.
    • The Oscillistone Prosthetic
    • Wondrous Item, Rare (Requires Attunement)
    • This mechanical arm is crafted from polished brass and damask steel, powered by a core of glowing, humming Oscillistones. It replaces a lost limb or can be worn as a heavy gauntlet over an existing arm.
    • Vibrational Force: Your unarmed strikes with this arm deal 1d8 bludgeoning damage plus 1d6 lightning damage. You can use your Intelligence modifier instead of Strength for the attack and damage rolls.
    • Structural Resonance: As an action, you can touch a non-magical object or structure and cause it to vibrate violently. If the object is a lock, hinge, or small fastening, it shatters or opens. If it is a large structure (like a wall), it takes 4d10 force damage, ignoring resistances.
    • The Siphon’s Spark: The arm has 3 charges. You can expend 1 charge to cast Lightning Bolt (DC 15). The arm regains all expended charges daily at dawn.
    • Curse: The Endless Hum. The constant vibration of the Oscillistones is taxing. While attuned, you have disadvantage on Stealth checks that rely on silence. Additionally, after a long rest, you must succeed on a DC 12 Constitution saving throw or gain one level of Exhaustion from the "phantom hum" keeping you awake.
    • Hapi’s Harpi
    • This magic harp can play music on its own without the need to pluck the strings. This harp has 3 charges. As a bonus action, you can spend 1 charge to cause the harp to play a powerful mind altering song for 1 minute. While playing this song, any creature of your choice that starts its turn within 15 feet of you, must make a DC 13 Wisdom Saving throw or be charmed or frightened of you (your choice). While charmed or frightened in this way, a creature can repeat the saving throw on the end of its turn, ending the effect on a success. Once a creature succeeds on this save, it is immune to this effect for 24 hours. The harp regains all expended charges at daily dawn.
    • Pink Pony Club:
    • You have advantage on Performance checks to dance while holding this club
    • This magic club can play music on its own. This club has 3 charges. As a bonus action, you can spend 1 charge to cause the club to play a powerful mind altering song for 1 minute. While playing this song, any creature of your choice that starts its turn within 15 feet of you, must make a DC 13 Wisdom Saving throw or be charmed or frightened of you (your choice). While charmed or frightened in this way, a creature can repeat the saving throw on the end of its turn, ending the effect on a success. Once a creature succeeds on this save, it is immune to this effect for 24 hours. The harp regains all expended charges at daily dawn.
    • This club deals an additional 1d6 psychic damage if you are actively dancing while attacking with it
    • As an action, you can transform the club into a Pink Pony (use the draft horse statblock) for 1 hour, after which it returns to being a club. You can do this once per day.
